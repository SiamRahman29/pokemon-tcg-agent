=== B8 rerun decision rule, written 2026-08-02 before the 16k A/B reported ===

Bar (unchanged): >= 0.541 at n=2000 vs the byte-identical control.

If it CLEARS      -> B8 lives; five-anchor sweep, then a submission decision.

If it NULLS, the question is whether to spend the now-feasible 40,000-game run.
Decide on the DIRECTION of the point estimate, not on wanting another go:

  4,000 games gave 0.512 [0.491, 0.534].

  (a) 16k point estimate materially ABOVE 0.512 -> more data is moving it;
      the 40k run is justified and gets the same bar.
  (b) 16k point estimate AT or BELOW 0.512 -> 4x the data moved nothing, so
      10x is not indicated. The axis closes on the method, not the budget,
      and B8 becomes a report chapter.

This is written down because "run it bigger" is the most available excuse after
any null, and the project has a rule about screening on the wrong thing.
Nothing here licenses a beta sweep either way.
