=== TRACK C DECK SEARCH -- pre-registration, written 2026-08-02 (day 18) ===
=== BEFORE any variant deck file existed and before any game was played. ===

WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
  §8al retired guess-a-swap: three user-proposed hunches -> one null, two
  significant losses, strength falling MONOTONICALLY with distance from the
  consensus 60. A search fixes the "hunch" half and makes the multiplicity half
  worse: k variants at alpha=0.05 manufactures a winner at k~20. That is the
  same shopping the B8 beta sweep was declined for (§8ao). So the candidate
  list, the screen, the confirmation and the kill line are all fixed here, in
  advance, and the generator cannot be retuned after seeing results.

PRIOR, STATED HONESTLY
  §8al measured the consensus 60 as a local optimum. The EXPECTED outcome of
  this search is a NULL. "A proper search over the slot space and the list
  survived it" is the Deck Score deliverable; it is not an Elo lever, and it
  will not be sold as one.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 1 -- SCREEN. RANKING, NOT TESTING.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  No p-value is computed at stage 1 and no candidate is called a winner here.
  Stage 1 exists only to order the list. This is what keeps stage 2 a single
  test.

  1a. MIRROR-SCREENABLE candidates (the cut card has §8ar spec <= 0.16, i.e.
      the mirror sees essentially all of its use). n = 4,000 games, DIRECT
      head-to-head variant-vs-stock (§8aj's design), which is the cheapest and
      only unbiased-by-construction cell we own.
      Reference: §8aj's same-deck control 0.4980 [0.483, 0.513]. NOT 0.500.

  1b. MIRROR-BLIND candidates (§8ar spec >= 0.5: Tool Scrapper 0.93, Froslass
      0.54). The mirror answers the wrong question BY CONSTRUCTION, so these
      skip 1a and screen at the two anchors where the card is most live:
      rule:alakazam5 (22.0% of the field) and rule:crustle (6.7%).
      n = 2,000 per arm. The stock-60 control arm at each anchor is measured
      ONCE and shared by every candidate there.

  Ranking statistic: estimated change in field-weighted W = sum_i w_i p_i,
  using §8ac weights, with unmeasured cells assumed 0.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 2 -- CONFIRM. ONE CANDIDATE, ONE TEST.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The single top-ranked candidate from stage 1 -- and only that one -- is run
  at p33's Neyman allocation over all seven anchors (57,600 games, ~1.1 h at
  rule 7's 2-3 jobs):

    mirror 15,800 | alakazam5 8,500 | archaludon 3,600 | crustle 2,700
    garchomp 2,200 | dragapult 2,000 | v10 1,900          (per arm)

  Resolution: +/-0.0050 on W.

  ⛔ If stage 2 is run on a candidate that was not stage 1's top rank, or on
     more than one candidate, this pre-registration is void and the result is
     a screen, not a test.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KILL LINE (user-approved 2026-08-02: "if the numbers say kill, kill")
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A variant SHIPS only if, at stage 2, it beats the stock 60 on field-weighted
  W with a 95% CI EXCLUDING ZERO.

  ⛔ A mirror-only win does not ship. §8ar measured why: 17 of 19 slots are
     mirror-safe but Munkidori (18.6 mirror vs 11.3-14.2 elsewhere), Impidimp
     and Morgrem are all played MORE in the mirror, so a mirror-only result
     overstates the core engine as much as it understates the tech.

  ⛔ No re-run at larger n after a null. §8ao's branch (b) is the precedent:
     "run it bigger" is the most available excuse after any null.

  ⛔ No second candidate promoted to stage 2 after the first fails. That is
     the multiplicity this design exists to prevent. If stage 1's leader dies
     at stage 2, THE SEARCH IS OVER and the consensus 60 stands.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE FROZEN CANDIDATE LIST -- 11 single-card swaps, all 1-for-1
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Generator, applied mechanically:
    * CUT candidates are ranked by §8ar weighted plays/game (what we can most
      afford to lose). ⚠ liveness caps what a swap can PAY; it is not a claim
      that the card is bad (§8e's Boss's Orders trap).
    * ADD candidates must pass §8af's exposure filter -- inside the corpus's
      134 card ids, at or above the exposure of the weakest card we already
      play (Tool Scrapper, 2,820 as our own option).
    * ALL swaps are 1-for-1. §8al's 2- and 4-card arms both lost and its
      4-card arm could not be attributed; §8ab's "derive and size, do not
      bundle" applies to decks too.
    * Excluded because already measured: Dawn -> 4th Marnie's Grimmsnarl ex
      (§8aj, null), Boss's Orders +1 (§6, null at 0.490), Budew lines (§8al).

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THE WHOLE DESIGN
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  If the stage-1 mirror control (stock vs stock) does not reproduce §8aj's
  0.4980 [0.483, 0.513], the harness has changed and every number below it is
  suspect. This is checked FIRST and nothing else runs until it passes.
